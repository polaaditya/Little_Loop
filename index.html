Who LittleLoop is for

Built around how real families live

No two families are the same. LittleLoop adapts to your schedule — not the other way around.

Working parents

Between back-to-back calls and late pickups, connection slips. One 60-second loop keeps you in your child's day even when you weren't physically there.

Stay-at-home parents

You're present all day, but "how was school" still gets a shrug. LittleLoop gives kids a low-pressure way to share on their own terms.

Distributed & blended families

Parents in different cities, grandparents across time zones. One shared loop keeps everyone in the circle.

A family-wide connection layer
